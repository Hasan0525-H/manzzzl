# Manzzzl AI Product Vision

## Core principle
The floor plan is the single source of truth.

## User flow
1. Login.
2. Upload floor plan.
3. Answer smart clarification questions.
4. AI validates understanding.
5. Generate digital house model.
6. Show realistic exterior render and 3D viewer.

## AI responsibilities
- Read architectural plans.
- Detect uncertain elements and ask questions.
- Apply Saudi architectural knowledge.
- Generate realistic materials and finishes.

## Restrictions
- No furniture in first version.
- No changing rooms or dimensions.
- No random additions unrelated to the plan.

## Target regions
- Jeddah.
- Abha.
- Mahayel Asir.
- Jazan.
